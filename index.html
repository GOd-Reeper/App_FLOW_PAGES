003 • FIELD OPERATING MANUAL

Practical Guide: How to Onboard Each Location

Exactly what time to visit, who to talk to, what to install, and the exact word-for-word pitch.

🏋️ Place 1: Commercial Gyms & Fitness Clubs
Highest Priority Target: 50 Gyms

⏰ Best Time to Visit

11:30 AM to 1:30 PM or 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM. The gym is empty between peak morning and evening workouts. The owner or general manager is sitting relaxed at the reception desk.

📦 Physical Asset to Install

  • Reception Counter: A5 High-Gloss Acrylic Standee with QR Code ("Download to Track Workouts").
  • Weight Room: Mirror sticker decals near dumbbell racks.
  • Locker Room: Door poster near water dispenser.

🤝 The Winning Deal We Offer

Give the gym owner free access to Trinity-Fit's Club Dashboard to track gym member attendance, plus a 10% referral cut on any personal training packages booked through their gym's QR code.

Exact Word-for-Word Pitch to Gym Owner:

"Hi Sir, we know personal trainers here use WhatsApp and paper diaries to manage client workouts, which looks unorganized. We built Trinity-Fit — a modern gym app. We want to give your gym free member management software. All we ask in return is to put this small premium acrylic standee on your reception counter. Whenever your members book a trainer on the app, we also share 10% revenue with your gym. Can we set it up on your counter today?"

🥗 Place 2: Healthy Cafes, Salad & Smoothie Bars
Diet Channel Target: 30 Cafes

⏰ Best Time to Visit

3:00 PM to 5:00 PM on weekdays (between lunch and dinner rush). Talk directly to the cafe owner or store manager.

📦 Physical Asset to Install

  • Dining Tables: Wooden base Table Tent QR cards.
  • Billing Counter: Bill-folder insert flyer with QR discount code.
  • Drink Coasters: Branded Trinity-Fit protein coasters.

🤝 The Winning Deal We Offer

We feature their cafe as a "Verified Healthy Food Spot" inside our app's diet section for free. In return, they keep our tent cards on their tables.

The Offer Printed on Table Card:

"Wondering how many calories & protein are in your meal today? Scan this QR code to calculate your exact daily calorie budget for free $\rightarrow$ Show your screen to the cashier to get 10% OFF your protein smoothie right now!"

💇 Place 3: Premium Salons & Aesthetic Spas
High Spenders Target: 20 Salons

⏰ The Secret Opportunity

Clients sit in salon chairs for 45 to 90 minutes getting hair coloring, keratin, or facials. They are bored, staring at their phones or table magazines, ready to read about fitness.

📦 Physical Asset to Install

  • Waiting Lounge: High-end laminated Lookbook on coffee table.
  • Mirror Stations: Discreet small QR tent card next to hair products.

🤝 The Winning Deal We Offer

Cross-promotion: Free 7-Day Personal Coaching voucher for every salon client spending over ₹2,000 on hair/skin services.

The Hook on the Salon Card:

"Getting ready for a wedding or vacation? Hair and skin look 10x better with a toned body. Scan here to hire top celebrity-certified fitness coaches from your city. First 7 days free trial."

🏘️ Place 4: Gated High-Rise Societies (The Sunday Camp)
Bulk Installs 50–150 Users per Camp

📅 When to Run

Sunday mornings, 7:00 AM to 11:30 AM near the society clubhouse or jogging track when residents come down for morning walks.

📦 Equipment to Carry

  • 1 Folding canopy table + 2 Trinity-Fit roll-up banners.
  • 1 Digital Body Composition Analyzer Scale (checks BMI, body fat %, muscle mass).
  • 100 free branded shaker bottles for participants.

🤝 How to Get Permission

Meet the RWA (Residents Welfare Association) Secretary on Thursday evening. Pitch it as a "Complimentary Community Health & Wellness Camp" (100% free, zero cost to society).

The Funnel at the Camp:

"Uncle/Aunty, please step on the body analyzer scale for your free health assessment. To see your full 6-page report including visceral fat and metabolic age, simply scan this QR code and download Trinity-Fit — your report opens instantly on your phone!"

Mass City Fame Months 2 & 3

✈️ Airports & Transit Lounges

Target busy business executives. Run 10-second vertical screen ads in departure lounge TVs: "Busy travel schedule? 15-minute hotel room workouts with personal coach accountability in your pocket."

004 • TRAINER RECRUITMENT

How We Onboard 10 Gym Trainers Every Week

Remember: 1 trainer brings 20 paying clients immediately. Here is the exact script and setup drill.

Step 1: The Floor Approach

Our sales rep walks up to a trainer resting between client sessions: "Bhaiya, we see you train clients here. You have great physique and knowledge. Do you train online clients as well?"

Step 2: Kill Their Pain Point

"How do you currently send diet plans and collect money? WhatsApp and GooglePay? What if clients forget to pay? What if you have 25 clients and WhatsApp becomes pure chaos?"

Step 3: The 10-Minute Setup

Sit with the coach right there on the gym bench. Take their best photo, client transformation picture, certification name, and monthly price (e.g. ₹3,500/month). Upload to app in 10 minutes.

The Exact WhatsApp Message the Coach Sends to His 20 Clients:

"Hey Rahul! From this week, I am upgrading our personal training to our new official coaching software on Trinity-Fit. All your workout logs, weekly weight check-ins, and diet charts are now tracked inside the app. Please click this link, download the app, and connect with me: [Coach Referral Link]. Let's crush this week's goals!"
